# 2-minute demo video

For the application (link it in the pitch deck and README) and as a backup at the City
Buildathon if the venue Wi-Fi fails.

## Setup

- Record the phone screen with Android's **Screen recorder** (quick settings), with sound on.
- Speak in **Marathi** in the app and narrate in **English or Hindi** (judges are national);
  add English subtitles if you can (YouTube auto-captions, then fix them).
- Charge the phone, turn on Do Not Disturb, use a real profile (your family's farm and crops).
- Open the app once a minute before recording so the server is awake.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**; put the link in the deck and README.

## Tips

- One take per section is fine; join the clips with any free editor (Google Photos, CapCut).
- Show real results only: if a photo isn't recognised, show the "not sure" screen. Judges like honesty.
- Keep it under 2:15.
