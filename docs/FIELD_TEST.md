# Field test: 5–10 farmers before 13 Nov

Goal: real evidence for the application and pitch deck. Judges score **market viability** and
**founder profile** on proof, and "10 farmers used it, here is what they said" is the strongest
proof a student team can have.

## Who to ask

- Family and neighbours who farm (start here: easiest yes).
- Farmers at the local APMC / mandi yard on an auction morning.
- A Krishi Sevak, agri-input shop owner or FPO member: one of them can introduce you to 5 farmers.

Aim for a mix: different crops (onion, soybean, tur, cotton, a fruit), different ages, at least
one farmer who mostly speaks Marathi and reads little.

## How to run each session (15–20 minutes)

1. **Ask first:** "मी एक शेतकऱ्यांसाठी ॲप बनवले आहे. १५ मिनिटे वापरून बघाल का? तुमचे नाव किंवा
   फोटो तुमच्या परवानगीशिवाय कुठेही वापरणार नाही."
2. Install the APK on **their** phone (or use yours), let them sign in and do onboarding
   **themselves**. Don't help unless they are stuck; note where they get stuck.
3. Ask them to try three things:
   - Home: "आज काय काम करायचे ते सांगते का?"
   - Mandi: their own crop's price, and *sell now or wait*.
   - Voice: ask one question by speaking (mic button).
4. Ask the questions below and write the answers in their words.
5. With permission, take one photo of them using the app (face optional).

## Questions (Marathi, with English)

| # | Question | What you learn |
| :-: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | आज शेतीचे निर्णय कसे घेता? (हवामान, भाव, फवारणी) कोणाला विचारता? | Today's alternative (competitor) |
| 2 | ॲपमधले कोणते एक काम सर्वात उपयोगी वाटले? का? | The killer feature |
| 3 | काय समजले नाही किंवा अवघड वाटले? | What to fix |
| 4 | मंडीचा भाव बरोबर वाटला का? तुम्ही कुठे विकता? | Data trust |
| 5 | आवाजाने विचारणे सोपे वाटले का? | Voice-first value |
| 6 | हे ॲप रोज / आठवड्याला वापराल का? | Retention |
| 7 | महिन्याला ₹२० / ₹५० द्यावे लागले तर? किंवा FPO / दुकानदाराने दिले तर? | Willingness to pay, who pays |
| 8 | अजून कोणत्या शेतकऱ्याला हे दाखवाल? | Word of mouth |

Keep names private: in the deck, use "a 45-year-old onion farmer from Niphad", not a name, unless
the farmer says it's fine.

## What to bring back

- The table above (a photo of a paper sheet is fine).
- 2–3 quotes in Marathi, word for word.
- Photos (with permission).
- Any FPO / Krishi Sevak / shop owner who said "we'd use this", with their WhatsApp message if possible.

The deck gets a slide with these numbers, for example: *"8 of 10 farmers found the mandi
comparison most useful; 7 would use it weekly; an FPO in Niphad asked for a pilot."*
