## Who you are designing for

You are designing a **10-slide** pitch deck (never more than 10) for **Arth (अर्थ)**, a phone app that tells smallholder
farmers in Maharashtra **what to do on their farm today**, in Marathi, Hindi and English.
It runs on live weather, live mandi prices, 40 years of government rainfall records and 9.26 lakh
Soil Health Card tests.

The deck is for **hackathon judges**. They will see a dozen decks in a row and give each a few
minutes. Every slide must land one idea in under 10 seconds: one headline, one big number or one
screen, and a short line of proof. Judges should leave having **felt who this is for** (real
photographs of farmers and their fields), **seen the real product** (the screens) and remembering
**three numbers**.

16:9, 1920 × 1080. Export as an editable PPTX and as PDF.

## What to borrow from the two attached decks

**From the RITU deck (our own previous deck): the way numbers do the talking.** Copy this closely:
- **Huge numerals** in the accent colour (about 120–200 pt), then a **one-line caption** under
  each in small grey body text. The number is the headline, and the caption explains it.
- **Rows of three stat blocks** separated by thin hairlines above each block (RITU slides 2 and 9).
- **Units belong to the number**: "40 yrs", "₹0", "86.7%", "2 of 36". Never a bare number.
- **A source line on every slide with data**, small and grey, bottom-left
  (e.g. "Source: IMD daily rainfall, 1985–2025").
- **Eyebrow labels** above each title: "01 · THE PROBLEM", in small spaced capitals, accent colour.
- **Footer** on every content slide: product name left, "03 / 10" right, both tiny and grey.
- **One colour "statement" moment** (RITU slide 4, the red one): huge text on a solid colour panel.
- **A before/after comparison card** (RITU slide 7, "Same farmer. A different answer."):
  a grey card for "other apps" → arrow → a white card with an accent border for us.
- **An "honest by design" slide** that separates **Measured** from **Estimated** (RITU slide 8).
- **A chart that tells the story without the legend** (RITU slide 3): the trend line is labelled
  in place ("His father's date · 27 June" / "The trend today · 14 June").
- **The closing slide** is a two-line punchline, the second line in accent colour, plus a pill with
  the live link.

**From the minimalist pitch deck: the calm, editorial layout.**
- Lots of white space; the content sits in the left 55–60% and visuals take the right side.
- **Bold sans-serif title + a short 48 px accent underline** below it (all slides).
- **Split layouts**: text on one half, a full-height image on the other: a real photograph of a
  farmer or field (see Photography below), or a phone screenshot on a soft tinted panel.
- **Photos that bleed to the slide edge**, with text set on a calm area of the photo or on an
  overlay (the minimalist deck's "Our Solution" and "Product Overview" slides).
- **Small icon tiles** in a grid for features (soft rounded squares, thin line icons).
- **Roadmap as tinted cards** that get deeper in colour from left to right.
- **A soft grainy gradient orb** bleeding off one edge of the slides that have no photograph.
- Accent-coloured page numbers.

**What NOT to copy:** the red/pink palette of either deck (Arth is green and gold), generic
office photography (laptops, desks, keyboards: our story is in the field, not the office), and the generic "Business Model" or "Market Opportunity"
filler slides, and the table-of-contents slide (no room in 10).

## Arth's visual identity

Use the app's own design tokens so the deck and the screenshots feel like one thing.

| Role | Colour | Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Background | `#E9E5DA` warm cream (or `#FAF8F3` for lighter slides) | every slide |
| Card | `#FFFFFF` | cards, phone backing panels |
| Primary: grow green | `#2E6B3F` (dark `#245231`, light tint `#E6EFE4`) | titles' underline, **"what Arth gives" numbers**, the statement slide background |
| Gold: the sun/coin from the logo | `#E8B04B` (deep `#C98F2E`) | highlights, the gradient orb, one key number per slide at most |
| Terracotta: risk/loss | `#C1531B` (tint `#FBEADF`) | **problem and loss numbers only** (days lost, fields deficient) |
| Ink / body / caption | `#17150F` / `#4A4638` / `#6E6A5E` | text |

**Colour has meaning; keep it consistent.** Terracotta numbers mean a problem the farmer faces.
Green numbers mean what Arth does about it. Gold is for the single number we want remembered on
that slide.

**Type:**
- Display / headlines: **Tiro Devanagari Hindi** (serif, the app's display face). It sets
  Devanagari and Latin in one family, so Marathi lines sit naturally next to English.
- Body, captions, numerals in stat blocks: **Inter** (tabular figures for numbers).
- Devanagari body text: **Anek Devanagari**.
- Never letter-space Devanagari: it breaks the conjuncts apart.

**Logo:** `logo.svg`: a sprout growing from a gold coin that is also a rising sun, over ploughed
furrows. "Arth" means wealth, meaning and purpose, so the mark reads as prosperity grown from the
land. Use it on the title and closing slides, and small in the footer.

**Signature motif:** a soft, slightly grainy **gold-to-green sun orb** (echoing the logo's
coin/sun) bleeding off the edge of the slides that have no photograph (3, 5, 7, 8, 9); the photo slides use the real sky instead.
Optionally, very faint **furrow lines** (three gentle arcs, as in the logo) at the bottom of
section slides. Keep both at 10–20% visual weight; they set the mood and never compete with the
content.

**Phone mockups:** present every screenshot in a clean, flat, modern phone frame (thin dark
bezel, rounded corners, soft long shadow), upright or tilted at most 6°. Place phones on a soft
`#E6EFE4` or white rounded panel. For callouts, use thin 1 px lines ending in a small dot,
pointing from a short label to the exact part of the screen. **Never crop away the part of a
screenshot that the slide is talking about.**

## Photography: make the judges feel who this is for

Real photographs carry the human side; the numbers and screens carry the proof. Use **5 photos in
total** (slides 1, 2, 4, 6 and 10), never on the transparency slide or the numbers slide.

**Style:**
- **Real, documentary photographs** of Indian, ideally Maharashtrian, farmers and fields: natural
  light, real clothes, real soil, real hands. Golden hour or overcast monsoon light. Warm, earthy colour
  that sits with the cream, green and gold palette (grade slightly warm, lower saturation).
- **Dignity, not pity.** Show a farmer who is thoughtful, working, deciding: never begging, crying
  or staged as a victim. The concern comes from the situation (a dry sky, a late monsoon, a
  pesticide label in small print), not from making him look helpless.
- People shot at eye level or slightly below, looking at the sky, the field or a phone, not
  posing at the camera.
- **Never AI-generated people.** Judges notice, and it breaks trust in a deck whose whole claim is
  "real data". If a real photo isn't available, use a field, sky or crop photo instead of a face.
- Only photos the team may use: their own photos, or free-licence sites (Unsplash, Pexels,
  Wikimedia Commons with a compatible licence). Put a tiny credit in the footer of each photo slide
  ("Photo: name / Unsplash").
- Text on a photo always sits on a gradient overlay (ink `#17150F` → transparent, or deep green
  `#245231` → transparent) so it passes WCAG AA, with the face never covered by text.

## Craft rules

- **One idea per slide.** Headlines ≤ 10 words, body ≤ 30 words. If it doesn't fit, cut it.
- Minimum 24 pt for body text, 14 pt for source lines. It must read from the back of a hall.
- A 12-column grid with 120 px side margins. Align every number block's top hairline across a row.
- Numbers use the Indian digit grouping where the figure is Indian-scale (9,26,473), with "lakh" in captions.
- Every screenshot slide must read even if the judge only looks at the phone: callouts name the feature.
- Real documentary photos only where this prompt places them (slides 1, 2, 4, 6, 10). No office
  stock photos, no AI-generated people, no emoji, no clip-art, no 3D icons. Thin line icons only, in the app's style.
- **Exactly 10 slides.** Do not add an agenda, divider, team or appendix slide.
- Consistent footer and slide counter on slides 2–9. Eyebrow numbering continues 01 → 08.
- Contrast at least WCAG AA on every text/background pair, including text on the green statement slide.
- Keep all charts, stat blocks and text **editable** (no flattened images of text).
